I am a Ph.D. candidate in [Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://www.ee.ucla.edu/) at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA), working in the [Bio- and Nano-photonics Lab](https://innovate.ee.ucla.edu/) under the mentorship of [Prof. Aydogan Ozcan](https://research.seas.ucla.edu/ozcan/). Before joining UCLA, I received my B.Eng. in Optical Science and Engineering from [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/) in 2021.

My research lies at the intersection of **machine learning**, **computational imaging**, and **optical systems**. I develop learning-based imaging and perception methods that jointly optimize physical hardware and computational algorithms, spanning computer vision, image processing, optical-digital co-design, and hardware-in-the-loop optimization. My work includes machine-centric imaging, computational imaging, optical neural networks, generative models, and reinforcement-learning-based optimization of physical systems.

My work has been published in journals including *Nature*, *Science*, *Light: Science & Applications*, *Advanced Photonics*, *Nature Communications*, and *Science Advances*, and has been cited over 1,300 times (h-index: 18).
