Terahertz waves penetrate many visually opaque materials, making them attractive for security screening and non-destructive inspection. With collaborators in the Jarrahi and Ozcan groups at UCLA, I work on **high-throughput terahertz imaging** (*Light: Science & Applications*, 2023), **rapid sensing of hidden objects with single-pixel diffractive terahertz sensors** (*Nature Communications*, 2023), and **detection and imaging of chemicals and hidden explosives** using terahertz time-domain spectroscopy and deep learning (*Light: Science & Applications*, 2026).
