Hi! My name is Yuhang Li (李煜航). I am currently a Ph.D. student at UCLA’s Bio- and Nano-photonics Lab, working under the supervision of Professor Aydogan Ozcan. I earned my Bachelor of Engineering in Optical Science and Engineering (with Honors) from Chu Kochen Honors College, Zhejiang University, China, in June 2021.

My research focuses on computational imaging and optical neural networks, particularly diffractive deep neural networks (D²NNs) and their applications in imaging through scattering media, all-optical classification, and complex field sensing.

selected publications

  1. Adv. Mater.
    All-optical complex field imaging using diffractive processors
    Yuhang Li*, Jingxi Li*, Yifan Zhao, and 4 more authors
    Advanced Materials, 2023
    Abs DOI Bib HTML

    Complex field imaging captures both amplitude and phase information of input optical fields or objects. Here, we present a complex field imager design that enables snapshot imaging of both the amplitude and quantitative phase information of input fields using an intensity-based sensor array without any digital processing. Our design utilizes successive deep learning-optimized diffractive surfaces that form two independent imaging channels performing amplitude-to-amplitude and phase-to-intensity transformations. We experimentally validated the efficacy through 3D-printed prototypes operating at the terahertz spectrum.
